The one rule that drives everything

Indiana gives new residents 60 days to convert both driver's licenses and to title + register both vehicles. Your clock starts at move-in (Jul 6), so the hard legal deadline is on/about Sep 4, 2026. Miss the vehicle deadline and it's a $30 penalty per car. Target mid-to-late July — don't wait.

01

Switch your car insurance — first

Do this before the BMV trip
🔑
Why it's step one: you must show Indiana proof of insurance to register the cars. No IN insurance = you can't finish at the counter. This gates everything else.
  • Call your insurer now. Give them the move date (Jul 6) and the new Indianapolis address.
  • It's a re-rate, not just an address change. Michigan is a no-fault/PIP state; Indiana is at-fault (tort), so the policy gets restructured. Your premium will very likely drop — Michigan is one of the priciest states in the country.
  • Make it effective on or just before Jul 6. Transition the same policy — don't cancel Michigan and leave a coverage gap.
  • Get new digital insurance ID cards showing the Indiana address to display at the BMV.

02

Driver's licenses — you + partner

The easy part if your MI license is valid
✅
No written test, no road test. Since you both hold valid MI licenses held over a year, you only need a vision screening. (Knowledge test applies only if expired; road test only if expired 5+ yrs or licensed under 1 yr.)
⚠️
You must surrender your Michigan license when you get the Indiana one — you can't hold both.

Bring these — and get the Real ID version (the star in the corner; it's now required to board domestic flights, and these are the exact documents it needs):

  • Identity + lawful status — U.S. passport or certified birth certificate
  • Full Social Security Number — SS card, W-2, or a pay stub showing the full SSN
  • TWO proofs of Indiana residency, dated within 60 dayse.g., signed lease + a utility bill (AES / Citizens), bank statement, or the insurance policy with the new address
  • Your current Michigan license

At the branch you'll get a 30-day temporary paper license; the real Real ID card arrives by mail.

03

Title + registration — both cars

Crosstrek + partner's vehicle

A VIN inspection is required for every out-of-state vehicle. Two ways to satisfy it:

  • Easiest & free: drive the car to the BMV branch — they verify the VIN on the spot at no charge. With two cars, just caravan both so both VINs get done in one visit.
  • Alternative: have a law-enforcement officer complete State Form 39530 beforehand (officer may charge up to $5) and bring it.

Documents per vehicle:

  • The out-of-state (Michigan) certificate of title
  • Indiana proof of insurance (from step 01)
  • Proof of Indiana address, dated within 60 days
  • SSN proof
🏦
Check whether either car is financed. If there's a loan, the lienholder holds the title — Indiana must request it from your lender, which adds lead time. If the Crosstrek or your partner's car has a loan, start this now, don't discover it at the counter.
🌿
No emissions test. Indiana only requires it in Lake & Porter counties — Indianapolis is Marion County, so you're exempt.

Ask for new-resident credit — Indiana may credit recently-paid Michigan registration, but they don't always offer it proactively.
04

Register to vote — just bundle it in

You can register to vote at the BMV during any transaction — no separate trip, no appointment needed. When you're doing your license, tell the clerk you also want to register to vote — or knock it out right now online at the official Indiana voter portal (you'll need your new IN license/ID, so easiest to do it after the BMV). (You must be a resident 30 days before an election to vote in it, but registering early costs nothing.)

06

One-trip BMV game plan

Tap to check off — saves automatically

Book an appointment at the BMV portal and find your nearest branch (walk-ins are accepted, but Marion County branches get busy and you've got 4 transactions). Bring both cars for the VIN checks. Order at the counter: licenses → VIN inspections → titles/registrations → "…and please register us to vote."

Progress saves automatically on this device.

07

Don't forget the Michigan loose ends

  • Your MI license is surrendered at the Indiana counter automatically.
  • Once Indiana plates are active, stop paying Michigan registration/insurance — let the MI policy roll into the IN one (no gap).
  • Retire the Michigan plates per MI's process once the Indiana plates are on the cars.
